## 1. Services

Provider will design, build, and deploy a website for Client consisting of:

- `[N]`-page static website based on Provider's `[category]` template
- Content population from Client's intake materials
- Mobile-responsive design, basic on-page SEO (titles, descriptions, sitemap),
  privacy policy and terms pages
- Deployment to Client's domain with SSL
- `[Optional: monthly care plan — hosting, DNS/SSL management, up to N small
  content edits/month, uptime monitoring — $X/month, cancellable with 30 days'
  notice]`

Anything not listed above is out of scope and handled under §4.

## 2. Price and payment

- **Total fixed price:** `$[amount]`.
- **Deposit:** 50% (`$[amount]`) due on signing. **Work does not begin until
  the deposit clears.**
- **Balance:** due on Client approval of the staging site, **before** the
  site is published to Client's domain.
- Invoices unpaid after 15 days accrue `[1.5]%` monthly late interest.

## 3. Timeline

Provider will deliver a staging link within `[5]` business days of receiving
**both** the deposit and completed intake materials. Delays in Client
providing content extend the timeline day-for-day.

## 4. Revisions and change orders

- The price includes **two (2) rounds of revisions** to the staging site.
  A round is one consolidated list of changes.
- Additional revisions, new pages, or new features are billed at
  `$[rate]/hour` or by written fixed-price change order.

## 5. Client responsibilities and content warranty

Client will provide accurate business information and any text, images,
logos, and other materials it wants used. **Client warrants it owns or has
rights to all materials it supplies** and will indemnify Provider against
claims arising from those materials. Provider warrants the same for
materials Provider supplies (templates, licensed stock imagery).

## 6. Approval and abandonment

If Client is unresponsive for `[30]` days at any stage, Provider may pause
the project; resuming may require a `[reasonable restart fee]`. Deposits are
non-refundable once template customization has begun, as they compensate
work performed.

## 7. Ownership

- On **full payment**, Client owns the final website content and design as
  deployed, and the domain name (registered in Client's name).
- Provider retains ownership of its underlying templates, tools, and
  processes, and grants Client a perpetual license to their use within the
  delivered site.
- Provider may display the completed work in its portfolio and place a
  discreet "Site by [Provider]" footer credit unless Client opts out in
  writing.

## 8. Hosting

`[If care plan:]` Hosting is provided under the care plan in §1. If the care
plan is cancelled, Provider will hand off the site files and DNS control
within 15 days of the final paid month. `[If no care plan:]` Client is
responsible for hosting; Provider will deploy to Client's chosen host at
handoff.

## 9. Warranties and liability

The site is delivered "as is" after the approval period. Provider does not
warrant specific business outcomes (rankings, traffic, revenue). **Provider's
total liability under this Agreement is capped at the total fees paid by
Client**, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

## 10. Termination

Either party may terminate on written notice. Client pays for work completed
to date; amounts already paid for completed phases are non-refundable.

## 11. General

Independent contractors; this is the entire agreement; amendments in writing;
governed by the laws of `[state]`, venue `[county, state]`.
